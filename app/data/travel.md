# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy FIN-014 | Version 3.2 | Effective October 1, 2026
Owner: Finance | Operational contact: Finance Operations | Review cycle: annual
Supersedes version 3.1 for transactions incurred on or after the effective date.

## 1. Purpose, scope, and precedence

This policy applies to employees of fictional Meridian Labs traveling on
approved company business, regardless of whether they use a corporate card or
personal funds. Contractors follow the reimbursement terms in their statements
of work; a hiring manager cannot promise employee benefits to a contractor.
Routine commuting, personal travel, and accompanying family costs are excluded.

Mandatory local law takes precedence where it conflicts with this policy.
Finance must document the applicable requirement and the affected claim.
Client reimbursement terms may impose lower limits but do not authorize higher
spend. A budget allocation or corporate-card limit is not spending approval.

## 2. Approval authority

Obtain trip approval from the line manager before booking. Calculate expected
trip cost across transport, lodging, meals, and booking fees, including taxes.
The trip approval and individual expense approval requirements both apply.

| Expected trip cost in USD | Required advance approvals |
| --- | --- |
| Up to and including $2,500 | Line manager |
| Above $2,500 through $7,500 | Line manager and cost-center director |
| Above $7,500 | Line manager, cost-center director, and Finance controller |

Manager approval is also required for each individual expense above $500 and
must be attached to the claim. An expense of exactly $500 does not trigger this
additional threshold. Split transactions do not bypass approval thresholds.
Reapproval is required before new commitments if the forecast moves into a
higher tier or increases more than 20% above the approved total.

No one may approve their own claim. If the traveler is the normal approver,
route to the next management level; the Finance controller's own claims require
CFO approval. Delegation must be recorded with a start and end date and cannot
transfer more authority than the delegator holds.

## 3. Booking, transport, and lodging

Book economy airfare and standard hotel rooms through the company travel
portal, normally at least 14 calendar days before domestic departure and
21 before international departure. Late booking requires a reason, not an
automatic rejection. Choose the lowest reasonable fare considering journey
time, necessary baggage, and change fees, rather than ticket price alone.

The nightly room limit is $220 before mandatory taxes. Finance may approve up
to $300 for a named high-cost destination or conference hotel before booking.
Amounts above $300 require the Finance controller's written exception.
Personal upgrades, minibar items, and leisure facilities are not reimbursable.

Business class requires advance Finance approval and is considered only for
scheduled flight time exceeding eight hours or an approved accommodation.
Medical details go to People Operations, not the expense claim. An
accommodation approval should identify the authorized booking condition only.

Use public transport or standard rideshare where practical. Rental vehicles
require a business justification; fines and penalties are personal expenses.
For mixed business and personal itineraries, attach a business-only comparison
quote captured at booking. Reimbursement cannot exceed the lower of actual
eligible cost and that comparison; personal extensions remain the traveler's
responsibility.

## 4. Meals, hospitality, and receipts

Employees may claim up to $65 for a travel meal, including tax and gratuity,
per person, per meal, with an itemized receipt. There is no daily allowance
that can be pooled across meals. The meal limit is a ceiling, not an entitlement.
Alcohol and guest meals are not eligible under this policy.

A meal included in a hotel rate, conference fee, or client event cannot also
be claimed separately. For a shared employee meal, one employee submits the
receipt, attendee names, and each person's allocation; other attendees must
not claim the same meal. Client hospitality requires separate Finance approval
under the hospitality process and cannot be reclassified as a travel meal.

A card statement alone is not an itemized receipt. For a lost receipt, request
a duplicate first, then submit a declaration with date, vendor, items, amount,
and business purpose. Finance reviews declarations individually. A missing
receipt does not waive a spending limit; declarations above $100 also require
controller approval. Three declarations within a rolling 90-day period trigger
a Finance review, not an automatic finding of misconduct.

## 5. Claims, currency, and reimbursement

Submit expenses within 30 calendar days of the transaction, with the business
purpose, cost center, receipt, currency, and required approvals. For a corporate
card, use the posted USD amount. For personal payments, use the bank's posted
conversion and attach evidence; if no conversion occurred, use Finance's
published rate for the transaction date. Do not claim both a converted amount
and the underlying foreign-currency amount.

Claims submitted on days 31-60 require the manager's explanation for lateness.
After 60 days, the controller must approve the delay unless local law requires
reimbursement. Finance returns incomplete claims with the missing items listed.
Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payment cycle after review; a
returned claim is not approved merely because the original deadline passed.

Refunds and unused-ticket credits must be reported within five business days
of receipt and linked to the original claim or corporate-card transaction.
Employees must not retain a refund for an expense already reimbursed.

## 6. Exceptions, emergencies, and records

Request exceptions from Finance Operations before commitment, identifying
the clause, amount, reason, alternatives, and dates. Finance may approve
exceptions within its delegated authority; the controller approves exceptions
to stated limits unless this policy assigns a higher approver. Approval applies
only to the named trip and does not establish a standing entitlement.

During an immediate travel safety disruption, employees may secure reasonable
transport or lodging without waiting for advance approval. Notify the manager
and Finance within one business day and submit the circumstances and receipts
within five business days. Finance reviews the claim after the event; this is
not a general waiver of excluded personal expenses.

Finance retains claims and approvals for seven years after the fiscal year
closes, subject to a longer legal hold. Suspected duplicate or altered claims
are referred for investigation; recovery or disciplinary action follows the
applicable employment process rather than automatic payroll deduction.
